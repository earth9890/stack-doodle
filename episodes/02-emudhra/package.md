## Title
eMudhra: The Indian Company That Almost Broke the Internet
(was: This company's secret key was literally the number 4; owner wanted it more clickbait, 2026-10-23; "almost" keeps it true: no misuse, browsers blocked in hours)

## Description
eMudhra's certificate authority keys explained: eight intermediate CA private keys were so weak (one was the number 4) that anyone could work them out from public certificates. Here's what went wrong, how it was caught, and what you need to update.

eMudhra, an Indian certificate authority trusted by every major browser, made the keys at a ceremony on 30 April 2025; a flaw produced a second set of weak keys, and the weak ones went to the browsers. A researcher spotted it on 17 Oct 2026; Mozilla, Chrome, Apple and Microsoft blocked the certificates within days. eMudhra pointed to its audits and to "social media amplification", its shares hit two 20% lower circuits (₹431.55 on 21 Oct, ₹345.25 on 22 Oct, BSE), and it is exiting the TLS business. Revocation doesn't reach software that never checks, so update your browser, OS, runtimes and certificate bundles.
