# topic: Google turns AI off by default for K-12 student accounts, and Search goes with it
# date checked: 2026-11-04   event date: Tue 2026-11-03 (blog.google post, 14:00 UTC; NYT interview)   format: Patch Notes
# the one-sentence story: Google gave schools an AI off switch, but Search counts as AI, so "no AI" means "no Google Search" for students.
# open questions: is it all-or-nothing? (no: per-product toggles, Google to Engadget; but Search has no AI-free mode) · when? (blog: "coming months"; NYT: early 2027) · can Search exist without AI? (Google's own 2024 Web filter shows no AI Overviews)

Primary: Chris Phillips (VP & GM, Education), "Designing responsible AI for learning", blog.google, Nov 3 2026.
Press (Nov 3): NYT (Goldstein & Singer, via Inquirer syndication), TechCrunch (Malik), Engadget (Holt), AP (Ortutay), Guardian (Bhuiyan),
Ars Technica (Whitwam), Quartz (citing Bloomberg + WSJ), 9to5Google, Futurism (opinion). Prior art: The Verge / TechCrunch / Ars (May 2024 Web filter).

## Confirmed (primary + 2 press)
- Blog: "Access to these products will be entirely off for K-12 student accounts until school administrators explicitly turn them on."
  Products: Gemini, Gemini Notebook, Gemini in Classroom, Google Search. Specifics "in the coming months".
- NYT/Phillips: "Search and AI are deeply integrated"; no-AI schools: students "will not have access to search, and they'll see a message
  from their school that indicates it's not available."
- Early 2027, two Search options for AI-enabled districts: consumer-style (AI Overviews + AI chats) or Guided Learning (NYT; 9to5Google).
- WSJ (via Quartz): no setting switches off Search's AI features alone.
- Engadget: Google says admins can enable products individually, "not an all-or-nothing deal". Ars says "no picking and choosing" → CONTESTED;
  script uses Google's per-product statement + "Search never comes without AI" (all sources agree on that part).
- August 2026: Gemini on by default in Classroom for schools that allowed student Gemini → backlash → now reversed (NYT, AP).
  AP: until August Gemini could be turned on only for students 18+.
- Sept 2026: NYC + LAUSD restricted student generative AI (NYT; AP: "temporary bans… in elementary and middle schools").
  LAUSD hoped for Search with AI Overviews off (NYT).
- Last week: NY State chatbot safety law for minors (NYT). Blumenthal + Hawley letter to Pichai, Nov 2 deadline (Quartz).
- Guided Learning: in Classroom globally from Nov 3; Search pilot "in select U.S. schools later this month" (blog).
  Google: "measurable learning gains" (blog); Bloomberg via Quartz: no independent evaluations cited.
  NYT reporter test: consumer Guided Learning wrote a detailed essay for an AP history prompt. School version: teachers can review transcripts (Mennuti, NYT; Engadget).
- 150 million students and teachers use Classroom (Google, via AP).
- Critics: Lina Khan ("using its search monopoly to coerce schools into adopting AI", AP/Guardian); Mayor Zohran Mamdani ("trying to use its
  monopoly power to force AI into our public schools", Guardian); Josh Golin / Fairplay (AP); Lila Byock / Schools Beyond Screens (Guardian).
- Ars: "Google could make a version of search for schools that doesn't have AI tools, but it's not doing that"; DuckDuckGo lets you disable AI.

## The turn (prior art)
- May 14 2024 Google SearchLiaison: "a new 'Web' filter that shows only text-based links". Google spokesperson Ned Adriance to The Verge:
  AI Overviews "will not appear when someone uses the web filter". Ars 2024: found "buried deep in the 'More' section".
  We do NOT claim the filter is still offered in Nov 2026 or that it would suit schools; script says "Google hasn't said".
